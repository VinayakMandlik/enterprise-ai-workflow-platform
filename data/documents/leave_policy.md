# Enterprise Leave Policy

Employees accrue 1.5 days of paid leave per month of service.
Unused leave up to 15 days can be carried over to the next calendar year.
Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 working days in advance
through the HR portal, except for medical emergencies.

## Sick Leave
Employees get 12 sick leave days per year. A medical certificate is
required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.
